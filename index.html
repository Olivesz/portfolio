chapter iii

freeflow

a second pass. Whisper re-reads the recent audio every 350 ms.

On-device dictation for macOS. Tap a key, talk, and your words are typed into whatever app has focus.

word errors: 6.7% live, 5.4% whole clip
20 held-out speakers · 19.7% before the fix

A message window fills in as someone talks. Two passes re-read the audio; a word is typed once both agree. Every pass is from a real pass log: freeflow’s streaming loop, whisper.cpp base.en, on a synthesized reading of Mark Twain’s coyote lines from Roughing It.

chapter iv

KRONOS

a planted answer. no estimator touches a real price until it finds one.

A regime-aware quant platform, every model hand-implemented in Python.

the result · risk control, not alpha

about equal-weight’s Sharpe, 64% of its drawdown

net of costs max drawdown Sharpe CAGR
KRONOS −18.7% 0.97 13.6%

Sharpe over cash; at rf=0 it is 1.12, 1.10 and 0.91 · in-sample on today’s large caps · up to ≤1.5× leverage

A self-audit found a sign bug all 30 gates then in CI had missed. The drawdown throttle braked at equity highs and released into crashes. The fix shipped with the missing gate.

A regime switch planted in synthetic returns; the fit locks onto it, and a slider moves it. Then the gates, then the run against SPY.

chapter v

custos-code

the log. the harness’s record of what the agent did.

Checks a coding agent’s final report against its own log, claim by claim. In opt-in auto mode, a contradicted claim keeps the agent from stopping and sends it back with a nudge.

six log sources · ten scenarios with known truth

2026 hackathon. Led a team of 3, designed the core engine.

The agent says the suite is green; its log shows failing tests: the claim is marked contradicted and the stop blocked. A passing run stops.
