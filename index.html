chapter ii · markets

kronos

Can markets be treated like physics? KRONOS is the lab I built to find out, every estimator hand-written on numpy. Net Sharpe 1.07 at −17.9% max drawdown (48 US names, 2013–2026, after costs) — the S&P's risk-adjusted return beaten at just over half its drawdown. One discipline holds it together: no estimator is trusted until it passes a gate whose answer is already known — 41 run in CI. And the selection risk is stated, not buried: the harness reports PBO 0.45 over 185 trials, meaning the edge over sibling configs is not certifiable.

Around it: an LLM ETF agent kept backtest-only until it survives the harness, and a Discord→broker copytrader behind a 13-point safety envelope.

chapter iii · voice

freeflow

Local speech-to-text that types into whatever has focus. Whisper on-device, streaming via LocalAgreement: two consecutive hypotheses are compared and only their agreed prefix ever commits. Words appear as you speak, and nothing you have already read is ever taken back.

Its siblings: casecraft speaks case interviews aloud and grades you like a real candidate. comic-reader hotkeys any manhua panel on screen and reads it back in the original, in the languages I grew up between.
